About Us

Connecting you to the UAE's best automotive specialists.

Who We Are

ScrapCar.ae is the UAE's premier directory for automotive spare parts, salvage yards, and specialized mechanic services. Finding the right part or a trustworthy garage in massive industrial hubs can be overwhelming. We created this platform to bridge the gap between consumers and verified local businesses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make car maintenance and repairs transparent, fast, and accessible. Whether you are looking for a rare pre-owned engine component in Al Sajaa or a top-rated paint shop in Sharjah, our live directory puts the contact information right at your fingertips.

Why Choose Us?

We believe in saving you time and effort. Instead of driving from shop to shop, you can browse our curated listings, view shop specialties, and contact them directly via WhatsApp or phone with just one click.
