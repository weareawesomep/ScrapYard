Safety Tips for Buying Used Parts

Buying salvaged or used spare parts is a great way to save money, but it pays to be cautious. Keep these basic tips in mind when dealing with scrap yards:

  • Verify Part Numbers: Always have your car's exact OEM part number or VIN ready to ensure compatibility. Visual matches can sometimes be deceiving.
  • Ask for Photos/Videos: Before driving to the shop, ask them to send a clear photo or video of the actual part via WhatsApp to confirm its condition.
  • Clarify Return Policies: Scrap yards operate differently than retail stores. Always ask if they offer a replacement guarantee or refund if the part turns out to be faulty upon installation.
  • Inspect Physically: Look for hairline cracks, rust, or broken mounting tabs before handing over cash.
  • Beware of Scams: If a price seems unbelievably low for a rare part, proceed with caution. Always try to deal in person for high-value items rather than sending money in advance.
