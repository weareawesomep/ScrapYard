Tips and guidelines for buying parts and services securely.

Your Safety is Our Priority

ScrapCar.ae provides a platform to connect with local automotive businesses, but your transactions happen directly with the sellers. To ensure a smooth and secure experience, please follow these basic safety guidelines:

Tips for Buying Used Parts

  • Inspect Before You Buy: Always check the physical condition of a used part before handing over payment. If purchasing an engine or transmission, ask if they provide a testing warranty.
  • Verify the Shop: Use the Google Maps links provided in our directory to ensure the shop is at a legitimate commercial location (like Al Sajaa).
  • Request Invoices: Always ask for a printed or digital receipt detailing the part purchased, the price, and any agreed-upon return policies.
  • Do Not Send Money Upfront: Avoid transferring money via unverified methods before seeing the part or confirming the shop's legitimacy.

Reporting a Business

If you encounter a fraudulent listing or a business that violates standard consumer practices, please let us know immediately via our Contact Us page. We actively review and remove bad actors from our directory.
