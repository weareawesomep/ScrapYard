Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and respect your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

ScrapCar.ae is a directory service designed to be used openly. We do not require users to create accounts, and we do not actively collect personally identifiable information (PII) from visitors simply browsing the directory. If you contact us via email, we will retain your email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Business Listings Data

The information displayed in our directory (Shop Names, Phone Numbers, WhatsApp links, and Locations) is sourced from publicly available data or submitted directly by the business owners. If you are a business owner and wish to update or remove your data, please contact us.

3. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party services, including Google Maps and WhatsApp. Please note that clicking these links directs you away from ScrapCar.ae. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms.

4. Cookies

We may use basic cookies or similar technologies to analyze site traffic and improve the user experience. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at mail2jaseel@gmail.com.
